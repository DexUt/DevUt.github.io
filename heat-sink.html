Experimental Investigation of Enhanced Heat Sinks

Controlling the temperature of onboard battery systems is a key thermal management challenge for hybrid electric aircrafts. Initially, a standard plate-fin heat sink was used in an innovative approach to dissipate heat generated by battery cells in electric vehicles. However, based on an analysis of a kW-scale hybrid electric aircraft, two heat sinks were specifically optimized for the aircraft's application: pin-fin and micro-structured heat sinks. This paper compares the performance of these optimized heat sinks with that of the original plate-fin heat sink.

Presented at: ASME Turbo Expo 2025

Authors: Faezeh Rasimarzabadi, Utkarsh Sheel Anand, Hassan Azarkish, Evgueni Bordatchev, Mahmood Shirazy

Paper ID: GT2025-151174

My Role

  • End-to-end contributor—from test-rig setup & instrumentation through data acquisition, thermal analysis, and manuscript prep.

Key Outcomes

  • At high airflow, the optimized pin-fin achieved up to +85% HTC vs. baseline.
  • At low airflow, the micro-structured sink was optimal and 27% lighter than the original.
